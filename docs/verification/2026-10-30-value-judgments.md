# Judged deck-building sessions, 2026-10-30

What this answers: are the server's deck-building ideas worth having, measured the same way three times across the day's fixes. Each session is a verbatim run through the public endpoint of a builder making a Cad Bane, Still Faster than You / Nevarro City, Restored deck against the current Premier meta, scored step by step by an adversarial judge (opus, no stake, same rubric each time: 0 misleading or empty, 1 correct but nothing new, 2 would influence a real card choice, 3 a non-obvious idea a strong builder acts on).

## TL;DR

Mean score moved from 1.09 to 1.25 to 1.50 to 1.64 to 1.57 to 1.57 to 1.71 of 3 across seven builds on the Cad Bane deck, then to 2.07 in the last of four reruns on 2026-10-31 and 2026-11-01 (1.79, 1.86, 1.93, 2.07). On the owner's own Warrior deck (session 8) it moved from 1.00 to 2.00 across twelve runs, with no step at 0 from run F on, and the share of steps a builder would act on from 2 of 11 to 11 of 14. The tournament-data tools (meta records, leader-ranked lists, `leader_cards` lift) earn their keep, and after wave 19 (#165) both decks' final runs reach VALUABLE, with the synergy steps scoring 2 or 3 on the Warrior deck. Neither margin is wide. The Warrior verdict at 2.00 sits exactly on the threshold and rests on one recalibrated step: its judge's own blind scale puts the run at 1.93, MIXED (judge-8-w19.md, session scratchpad, not committed). The Cad Bane verdict at 2.07 would be exactly 2.0 if one step scored a point lower (judge-7-w19.md, session scratchpad, not committed). The sections below keep each run's reasons.

## What the sessions established

- Every tool answered every step with no `isError` across the seven runs (93 calls).
- The pairs-v1 benchmark moved only upward through the matcher changes: 0.7424 to 0.7462 to 0.7617 to 0.7656 overall, 0.7048 to 0.7091 to 0.7267 to 0.7313 mechanical, recall of labelled-correct pairs 147 of 147 throughout.
- Complaints confirmed resolved by the third judge: leader-aware weighting, no Porg or unplayed 9-to-11 fills, cost-banded budget swaps with correct savings, rotated-set wording, no leaders as partners, honest evidence classes, pinned cluster labels, the token-Creature want-side check.

- The waves aimed at the Warrior deck reached the Cad Bane deck late: the first Cad Bane rerun (judge-7-w15.md) found the synergy steps almost word for word as in session 7, and the larger gains followed the w16 and w18 harness changes and the #162, #164 and #165 fixes.
- Both decks' final runs reach VALUABLE (Warrior 2.00, Cad Bane 2.07) on thin margins: the Warrior verdict rests on one recalibrated step (see the note after run L above). The Warrior judge names steps 6 and 7 (trio fillers, the audit's three-cost glut) as the remaining headroom; the Cad Bane judge names the Oggdo Bogdo parse (step 5) and leader-anchored trios (step 14) (judge-8-w19.md and judge-7-w19.md, session scratchpad, not committed).
