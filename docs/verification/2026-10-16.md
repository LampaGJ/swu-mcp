# Verification of the derived data, 2026-10-16

Last updated 2026-10-16.

## TL;DR

The deterministic audit passes: 9 of 9 checks, 0 failures, 146,827 assertions. It proves the data products match their originals (raw pages, folding, card fields, prices, indexes, rulings, play stats, glossary coverage, rules sections). It does not prove the meaning layers are right. The sampled review of those layers found errors.

Of 295 sampled items, 208 were judged correct by the haiku reviewers, 52 wrong, and 35 unsure. A second pass (sonnet adjudication against the originals) kept 46 items as wrong, overturned 39 haiku flags (a 44.8% false-positive rate among the 87 flags), and left 3 unsure. A spot check of 15 of the 208 "correct" items overturned 1. The overall corrected error rate is 15.8% (Wilson 95% interval 12.0% to 20.4%), a floor, because 193 correct items were not re-checked.

The errors cluster in the meaning layers: pairings (35.9%), features (27.5% and 22.5%), and fungible classes (16.0%). The rules text, the glossary and the field guide are mostly sound (rules 0 errors in 40). The two biggest root causes are conditions left as residue (the effect reads as unconditional) and a pairing step that ignores trait, arena and ownership qualifiers.

## Part A: deterministic audit

Source: `data/runs/2026-10-16/verify/audit.json`. All nine checks passed with 0 failures.

- raw-pages: sha256 and byte length of each page against the manifest; record count equals the API total (628 assertions).
- folding: each printing sits in exactly one logical card, the canonical printing follows the rule, and the JTL+ filter is exact (22,451).
- cards-fields: every `cards.toon` field equals the canonical raw printing's attribute (58,010).
- prices: `cards.toon` and `market/prices.json` equal the `tcg_printings` rows (33,453).
- indexes: every facet index's members and counts equal a recomputation from `cards.toon` (3,889).
- rulings: card rulings equal the raw `rules` and `rulesStyled` fields, dates and text (5,349).
- play-stats: `market/play.json` equals the pinned SWU Stats capture rows (16,683).
- glossary: every facet value in the data has an entry, and no entry is an orphan (749). This checks coverage only, not what each definition says.
- rules-sections: section text in order reproduces the `pdftotext` body, ignoring whitespace (5,615).

Notes from the audit:

- Weekly Play rule: 35 excluded cards carry a Weekly Play variant on a year-1 set (SOR, SHD, TWI). The filter counts the "<set>P" expansion codes, not the variant name. The owner said Weekly Play means JTL and later, so these are excluded by design.
- Dropped plain rulings: 38 printings have plain `rules` lines that appear in no styled ruling of the same card, so they are not in `card-rulings.json`. Filed as #10.

## Part B: method

1. Seeded sampling. `scripts/verify/sample.ts` (seed `verify-2026-10-16`) ordered every item by sha256 of the seed plus the item id and cut 8 packets: features-1 and features-2 (40 cards each, stratified by type and set), curated (40 entries), pairings (40 pairs), fungible (25 classes), rules (40 sections), glossary (40 entries), fieldguide (30 entries). That is 295 items. Each item carries the original text beside the derived claim.
2. Haiku verdicts. Eight haiku agents each judged one packet from the inline evidence only, and returned correct, wrong or unsure per item. Result: 208 correct, 52 wrong, 35 unsure (counted from the verdict files; the task brief said 207).
3. Sonnet adjudication. Each of the 87 wrong and unsure items was re-judged against the packet and, where the packet lacked evidence, the originals: raw card text in `swu-originals/2026-10-16/swu-cards/raw-pages/`, the price files, the catalogue snapshots, the rules PDF and `rules/sections.json`, the glossary, `features.json`, and the grammar code. Parser claims were reproduced by running `parseTarget` and `parseClause` on the quoted text. Final verdicts: confirmed-wrong, haiku-error (the data is right), still-unsure.
4. False-negative spot check. Taking the correct items in packet order (features-1, features-2, curated, pairings, fungible, rules, glossary, fieldguide), every 14th starting at the first gave 15 items. Each was re-read against the evidence and the source.
5. Rates. Corrected error rate per packet is confirmed-wrong divided by judged items. Still-unsure items are left out of the denominator and listed separately. Intervals are Wilson 95%, computed by `tmp/verify/adjudicate.mjs`.

Limits. The sample is stratified, so a packet rate describes the sample, not every card. The verdict reasons do not list each tag checked, so tag precision (supported tags over tags checked) cannot be computed; every rate below is item level. The rates are floors: only the 87 flagged items and 15 sampled correct items were re-checked.

## Root causes, ranked

Ranked by confirmed count, then by how many outputs the defect reaches. "Reach" figures are counted from `features.json` and the tag lists, not estimated.

### 1. RC06 Pairing ignores trait, arena, ownership and trigger qualifiers (6 confirmed)

- What and how far it reaches: Six pairs (of 14 wrong pairs). The pairing step is a set intersection of offers and wants tags. A tag carries no trait, arena, ownership or trigger qualifier, so 'Bounty Hunter', 'Ground', 'Rebel', 'non-Vehicle', 'while attacking' and 'enemy Credit token' are all lost.
- Outputs affected: find_pairings, find_trios, explain_synergy (the pair list and the printed reason). The reason text is the row text, so it can be false for the pair.
- Where: src/synergy/graph.ts:99-114 (pairing), src/ability-grammar/tags.ts:62,88-91 (tags that drop the qualifier).
- Proposed fix: Carry an optional `scope` on the tag (arena, trait list, side) and have graph.ts drop a pair when the offer scope cannot satisfy the want scope. Start with arena and trait, which the six pairs show.
- Items: 09010094|04010033|sac-outlet-when-defeated; 08010110|06010013|self-defeat-defeat-watcher; 07010232|07010106|credits; 07010061|07010074|ready-reuse; 09010078|07010005|self-defeat-defeat-watcher; 09010037|04010062|heal-payoff

### 2. RC13 Conditions are left as residue, so the effect reads as unconditional (6 confirmed)

- What and how far it reaches: Six sampled items. The widest reach: 330 of 2,434 clauses (13.6%) in `features.json` still carry residue words, 173 of them contain an if or while, across 157 cards. The effect is stored with no condition, so a reader sees an unconditional effect.
- Outputs affected: card_features, find_by_effect (an effect is returned although its condition failed), and every tag built from effects (find_pairings, find_trios, fungible_substitutes).
- Where: src/ability-grammar/grammar.ts:283 (`ref_check`) and the condition block at :263-289.
- Proposed fix: Add condition rules for the recurring shapes: 'if its cost is N', 'if it shares a trait', 'if attached unit is X', 'if it's not a unit', 'if it's returned'. Until then, mark an effect `conditional: true` whenever a clause has an if or while in its residue, so tags can be down-weighted.
- Items: 08010042; 07010225; 09010148; 07010111; 05010187; 0201057

### 3. RC15 Phrasing variant not matched by any rule (4 confirmed)

- What and how far it reaches: Four items. Phrasings no rule matches: 'for every N', 'play it', 'ignoring 1 of its ... penalties', and a token whose whole text is in brackets.
- Outputs affected: card_features and any tag built from the missing effect.
- Where: src/ability-grammar/grammar.ts:233,282,284 and src/ability-grammar/segment.ts:47.
- Proposed fix: Add the four phrasings as rules; stop stripping a paragraph whose entire text is a parenthetical.
- Items: 05010100; 09010150; 05010t03; 07010020

### 4. RC03 Resources are treated as units or cards (3 confirmed)

- What and how far it reaches: Three items, plus the shared tie-break defect. Resource cards are treated as units or cards: 'exhaust 2 enemy resources' (4 cards), 'ready each card resourced' (3 cards), 'defeat a friendly resource' (4 cards). The exhaust rule loses to the generic rule because equal-length matches resolve by production order.
- Outputs affected: find_by_effect (offers:exhaust-enemy, offers:ready-unit, offers:defeat-friendly-unit and wants:sac-fodder), fungible_substitutes classes, find_pairings.
- Where: src/ability-grammar/grammar.ts:204-206 and :335 (tie-break), :19 (no resource kind), src/ability-grammar/tags.ts:14-15,87,127-128.
- Proposed fix: Add kind `resource` to `parseTarget`, prefer the more specific production on a tie, and have the tag cases skip kind resource.
- Items: 07010013; offers:ready-unit|Unit|once_on_play|m1|c6+; offers:exhaust-enemy|Event|once_on_play|m1|c2-3

### 5. RC12 Target kind comes from the first noun in the phrase, not the head noun (3 confirmed)

## Found while checking, not counted above

- A "while you control ..." clause never becomes a typed condition. `while_generic` (`grammar.ts:288`) starts earlier and is longer than `control_n` (`grammar.ts:265-267`), so it wins. 40 clauses on 39 cards lose their wants tags (for example Hunting Nexu loses wants:trait-Aggression).
- `wants:replay-when-played` (`tags.ts:59`) is on 334 cards: every unit with a When Played. That is by design, but it means the bounce-replay row pairs a bounce with almost any unit, so its score carries little information. The why text 'When Played on a unit' reads like a condition and is not one.
- Glossary trait counts (Rebel, Vehicle and the others) count all sets and all printings, which is literally what the sentence says. The entries also carry `inScope: true`. A reader will take the counts as in-scope counts (Rebel is 155 cards in scope, 228 across all sets). Say 'across all sets' in the sentence.
- Field-guide counts mix two populations: the 10,193-printing snapshots (deployBox 513, artist 199) and the 10,246-printing current pages (515 and 201). The entries say which snapshot, but the guide describes the current data.
- The glossary trait Vehicle text count (136) does not reproduce (138 or 134, depending on the card key). No generator script for the glossary is committed.
- Packet-builder defect: `scripts/verify/sample.ts:514-535` cuts a field-guide body at the next bullet, so a parent entry such as import.counts shows 'Its children:' and none. Fix before the next run.
- The audit's glossary check (749 assertions) tests that every value has an entry. It could not catch Coordinate, Event or Ground. Add a check that each definition's counts equal a recomputation.
